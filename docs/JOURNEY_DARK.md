# Journey (Case Path) — dark mode redesign

Reference: the dark mockup shared on 2026-10-31 (moody, candle-lit law-library look).
Light mode does not change.

## 1. What is different today, and what changes

| # | Area | Today (dark mode) | Reference | Change |
|---|------|-------------------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Paintings | The light-mode watercolours (pale, airy, made for a cream page) are reused. ## 2. Picture prompts

**Deliver each as:** PNG, the size given in the prompt, **solid background in exactly `#1C1713`**
(the app's dark background; no transparency needed). Send the PNGs; they are converted to `.webp`
and saved as `assets/images/<file name>.webp`. Each prompt below is complete on its own:
paste one prompt, get one picture.

Priority: 1, 2, 3, 13, 14 are on the first screen; then 4–5; the rest can follow.

**Added 2026-10-31:** all 14 are in `assets/images/` (840 px wide paintings, 456 px wide strips,
WebP). Prompts 4, 5, 8 and 9 came back with the subject on the side that fades into the road,
so they were mirrored left to right before saving; none has readable text. The foliage strips
have their inner-side fade (opaque to 45% of the width, transparent by 85%) saved in the files.
